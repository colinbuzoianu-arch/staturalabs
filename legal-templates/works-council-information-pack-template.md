## Purpose
This pack gives the works council (Betriebsrat / Arbeitnehmer:innen-Vertretung)
the information it needs to exercise its participation rights when the employer
introduces and uses the Statura Labs Dynamics platform. It restates
already-reviewed positioning; it does not introduce new legal assertions.

## 1. What the system is (and is not)
- A workplace **risk-assessment and environmental-protection** platform that
  produces the legally required documents and proves corrective measures worked.
- It **does not evaluate, monitor, score or rank individual employees.** The
  assessment data is about **workstations, tasks and conditions**, not people.
- **No camera and no video** anywhere in the product. No image, frame or footage
  is captured, transmitted or stored (removed for data-protection reasons).
- Posture is entered by an assessor as an **observed category**, not measured
  from a person.

## 2. What personal data is held, and why
- The **assessment chain is person-free by construction** (see the TOMs
  document): no worker identity sits anywhere near a workstation, task or score.
- The only personal data is the **statutory people records** the law itself
  requires: instruction/training proof, incident and first-aid records, and
  statutory notifications. These live in a separate compartment with no link
  into the assessment data.
- Statistics about people pass a **minimum group-size gate** so no figure can
  identify or rank an individual.

## 3. Health data (incidents)
- Health detail exists only for injured persons in incident records, is
  access-restricted to the occupational physician, is **encrypted**, and every
  access is logged. It is never shown in statistics, documents or exports beyond
  the coarse injury type the law requires.

## 4. Participation and co-determination
- This pack is provided to support the works council's information and
  consultation rights; the employer remains responsible for the consultation
  process itself.
- A works-council member can be given a **read-only** platform role that excludes
  health data.

## 5. Data-protection summary
- EU hosting; access control, audit logging, retention limits and deletion on
  contract end (see the TOMs document for specifics).
