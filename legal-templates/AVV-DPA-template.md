## Parties
- **Controller** (customer): [legal name, address, representative]
- **Processor**: Verumsell SRL (product: Statura Labs Dynamics), [address,
  representative]

## 1. Subject matter and duration
Processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in the
course of providing the Statura Labs Dynamics occupational-safety and
environmental-protection platform. Duration: the term of the main services
agreement; this DPA ends when all personal data has been returned or deleted per
clause 9.

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing
Hosting and operation of the platform: workplace risk and environmental
assessments (person-free), and the statutory people records the product holds —
instruction/training proof, incident and first-aid records, statutory
notifications. See the TOMs document for the compartment separation.

## 3. Categories of data subjects
- Platform users (customer staff with login accounts).
- Employees recorded for statutory duties (training proof, incident involvement,
  first aid). **Not** the subjects of the (person-free) assessment data.

## 4. Categories of personal data
- Contact/identity of platform users and recorded employees (name, work email,
  org unit).
- Training and instruction records.
- Incident/accident records, including **special-category health data** (Art. 9)
  for injured persons, held encrypted and access-restricted (see TOMs §2).

## 5. Processor obligations (Art. 28(3))
- Process only on documented instructions from the Controller.
- Ensure persons authorised to process are bound by confidentiality.
- Implement the technical and organisational measures in the attached **TOMs**
  (Art. 32).
- Engage sub-processors only per clause 6.
- Assist the Controller with data-subject requests and with Art. 32–36 duties,
  taking into account the nature of processing (the platform's export and
  deletion functions support this).
- At the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data at end of
  provision (clause 9).
- Make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance and allow
  audits.

## 6. Sub-processors
General written authorisation applies; the Processor informs the Controller of
intended changes and gives the Controller the chance to object. Current
sub-processors: see `subprocessors-template.md`.

## 7. International transfers
[State hosting region and whether any transfer outside the EU/EEA (or outside
Switzerland for CH customers) occurs; if so, the transfer mechanism (SCCs, etc.).
Default deployment: EU hosting — confirm per deployment.]

## 8. Data-subject rights and breach notification
The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of
a personal-data breach, with the information needed for the Controller's own
notification duties.

## 9. Return and deletion
On termination, the Processor deletes or returns all personal data per the
Controller's instruction, including existing copies, unless retention is legally
required. The platform's contract-end deletion and export functions implement
this (see TOMs §5, §6 of the plan).
