## Clause (draft)

**Prüf- und Vorschauberechnungen / Preview calculations.** The platform may show
calculated or estimated values for hazardous-substance mixtures (for example an
acute-toxicity estimate, physical-property estimates, a water-hazard-class or
storage-class suggestion, or a draft safety data sheet). Where such a value is
marked as a *Vorschau-Berechnung* / preview (including any output carrying the
notice *„Vorschau-Berechnung — nicht fachlich geprüft"* or a document watermarked
*„ENTWURF — NICHT ZUR WEITERGABE"*):

1. it is provided **only as a checking aid** to compare against a supplier's
   safety data sheet, and is **not** a classification, labelling, a safety data
   sheet, or legal advice;
2. it has **not** been reviewed or validated by a qualified person, and must
   **not** be used for classification or labelling under CLP, for a safety data
   sheet sent to a third party, for the statutory substance register
   (Gefahrstoffverzeichnis), or for an operating instruction (Betriebsanweisung);
3. the customer remains responsible for the correctness of its own classification
   and documentation and for obtaining qualified advice; and
4. the provider gives no warranty as to the accuracy of a preview value and
   accepts no liability arising from its use contrary to (1)–(3).

A calculated value may be adopted into authoritative records **only** after the
relevant engine has been reviewed and confirmed by a qualified person (the
platform's VALIDATED state, guarded by a recorded expert review).
